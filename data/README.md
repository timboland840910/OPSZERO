# Data Folder

This folder contains important resources related to our data models, schemas, and storage configurations. Here's a brief overview of what you can find inside:

## Data Models

- **Data Models** provide the structure and organization of the data used in our applications. They define how data is stored, organized, and accessed.

## Schemas

- **Schemas** specify the rules and structure for the data being stored in our databases. They include definitions for tables, fields, and relations between different entities.

## Storage Configurations

- **Storage Configurations** outline how and where our data is being housed, including details about the databases in use and configurations necessary for optimal performance.

By maintaining clear documentation in this folder, we ensure that our data infrastructure is comprehensible and easily navigable for development and operations.
